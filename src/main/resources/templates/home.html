Proper Education and Quality Knowledge

Education, Training and Research Unit (ETRU)

The Education, Training and Research Unit (ETRU) of the National Blood Center (NBC) plays a crucial role in enhancing the knowledge, skills, and professional competencies of blood bank personnel across the country. Committed to upholding high standards in Transfusion Medicine, the ETRU facilitates Continuous Professional Development (CPD), in-service training, and cutting-edge clinical research initiatives.
