Preparation & Guidelines

Information For Donors

Everything you need to know before, during, and after your life-saving blood donation.

Basic Eligibility Criteria

To ensure both your safety and the safety of the patient receiving your blood, please check if you meet these basic requirements.

cake

Age

Between 18 and 55 years old (Up to 60 years for regular donors).

scale

Weight

Your body weight must be at least 50 kg (110 lbs).

favorite

Health

Must be in good general health and feeling well on the day of donation.

update

Frequency

Must wait at least 4 months (120 days) since your last whole blood donation.

The Donation Process

The entire process takes about 45 minutes, while the actual blood draw only takes 8-10 minutes.

1

Registration

You'll present your NIC/Passport, read information about blood donation, and fill out a confidential medical history questionnaire.

2

Mini-Medical

A medical officer will check your blood pressure, pulse, weight, and do a quick finger-prick test to ensure your hemoglobin levels are safe.

3

Donation

You'll rest comfortably on a donor bed. A sterile, new needle is used for every donor. About 450ml of blood is collected safely.

4

Refreshments

You will be asked to rest in the observation area for 10-15 minutes while enjoying provided snacks and liquids before leaving.

medical_information After Care Advice

  • water_drop Drink plenty of extra non-alcoholic liquids for the next 24 hours.
  • restaurant Eat a healthy meal after donating. Do not skip meals.
  • fitness_center Avoid heavy physical activities or lifting heavy weights for the rest of the day.
  • healing Keep the bandage on for a few hours. If bleeding occurs, raise arm and apply pressure.
